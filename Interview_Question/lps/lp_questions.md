# List 1: General behavioural questions

1. Tell me about yourself.
2. Tell me about your most challenging project and your contribution.
3. Tell me about a mistake or failure and what you learned.
4. Tell me about a time you helped a struggling teammate.
5. Tell me about a disagreement with a teammate or manager and how you resolved it.
6. Tell me about a time you missed a deadline or worked under tight constraints.
7. Tell me about a time you received negative feedback and how you responded.
8. Tell me about a time you made an important decision with limited information.
9. Tell me about a time you came up with an innovative solution.
10. Tell me about a time you faced ambiguity or changing requirements.
11. Tell me about a time you took ownership beyond your assigned responsibilities.
12. Tell me about a time you had to learn a new technology or overcome a technical challenge.

# List 2: Leadership Principles (Amazon-focused)

1. Customer Obsession: Tell me about a time you solved a customer problem.
2. Ownership: Tell me about a time you took responsibility beyond your role.
3. Bias for Action: Tell me about a time you made a quick decision without consulting your manager.
4. Dive Deep: Tell me about a time you investigated a complex technical problem or production issue.
5. Invent and Simplify: Tell me about a time you introduced an innovative solution or simplified a process.
6. Earn Trust: Tell me about a time you earned a colleague's trust or helped someone succeed.
7. Have Backbone; Disagree and Commit: Tell me about a time you challenged a decision or defended an unpopular idea.
8. Deliver Results: Tell me about a time you achieved an important result despite obstacles or tight deadlines.
